Connecting compassion with people who need it most

HopeBridge is a community-driven fundraising platform created to connect generous people with individuals and families facing difficult medical and financial circumstances.

We believe no one should face a serious health crisis alone simply because they cannot afford the care they need. Through verified fundraising campaigns, we bring attention to genuine needs and give people around the world a way to make a real difference.

Our mission

To make giving easier, more transparent, and more impactful. We connect donors with verified cases, give clear information about each need, and help make sure support reaches the people who need it.

Transparency

We encourage clear documentation and verification for every campaign.

Compassion

Every patient and family deserves to be treated with dignity and respect.

Community

Small acts of generosity create meaningful change when people come together.

Impact

We focus on helping donations contribute toward real, documented needs.

Who your gift reaches

We support patients who are turned away or go into debt because they can't afford the care they need.

Emergency and surgery

Operations and emergency treatment that can't wait for funds.

Medicine and ongoing treatment

Long-term medication and follow-up visits for chronic conditions.

Mothers and children

Safe delivery, newborn care and treatment for sick children.

Tests and diagnosis

Lab work and scans, so treatment can start without delay.

How HopeBridge works

From a verified need to your gift, every step is meant to be clear.

  1. A need is verified

    Each campaign is backed by documentation of the medical need.

  2. You choose

    Read the case and see exactly what the campaign is raising for.

  3. You give

    Donate with Sentz today, with more options on the way. Small gifts add up.

  4. We pay the provider

    HopeBridge pays the hospital, clinic or pharmacy directly for the documented care.

Questions donors ask

Which payment methods can I use?

Right now, Sentz, which works worldwide. Flutterwave and PayPal are coming soon.

How do I know my Sentz gift arrived?

Sentz shows your payment as sent. Fill in the short form under the Sentz tab and we will email you to confirm.

Can I give every month?

Yes. Send a Sentz payment each month. Monthly giving on Flutterwave and PayPal will come when they launch.

Who receives my gift?

HopeBridge pays the hospital, clinic or pharmacy directly for the documented need. Money is not handed to individuals.

Is my gift tax-deductible?
